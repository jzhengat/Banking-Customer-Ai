Savings Account:
You can open a savings account with valid ID proof and address verification.

Loan Application:
Loan processing takes 3–5 business days for approval.

Credit Cards:
Credit cards are issued based on income and credit score.

ATM Issues:
If your ATM card is blocked, contact customer support or use the app to unblock it.

Ticket Support:
Customers can track issues using a ticket ID in the support system.
